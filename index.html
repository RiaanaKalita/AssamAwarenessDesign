02 / PREPARE

Small steps. Safer choices.

Knowing what to do can help you and your family prepare for a flood.

01⌂
BEFORE A FLOOD

Prepare early.

  • Follow official weather and flood warnings.
  • Prepare a bag with drinking water, food, medicines and a torch.
  • Keep important documents protected and phones charged.
  • Know your family's evacuation route and meeting point.
02↗
DURING A FLOOD

Stay alert.

  • Move to higher ground when authorities advise you to.
  • Never walk, swim or drive through floodwater.
  • Stay away from swollen rivers, drains and fallen power lines.
  • Follow evacuation instructions and seek help when needed.
03✳
AFTER A FLOOD

Return carefully.

  • Return only when authorities say it is safe.
  • Avoid damaged buildings and electrical equipment exposed to water.
  • Drink safe water and avoid food contaminated by floodwater.
  • Report hazards and seek medical help if you feel unwell.
In danger? Contact emergency services and follow local official instructions.
