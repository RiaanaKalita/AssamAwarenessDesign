02 / PREPARE

Know the steps.
Protect each other.

Simple actions to help you prepare for a flood and respond safely.

01⌂
BEFORE A FLOOD

Prepare early.

  • Follow official weather alerts and flood warnings.
  • Prepare water, food, medicines, a torch and essential documents.
  • Charge phones and keep emergency contacts accessible.
  • Plan a safe route and meeting point with your family.
02↗
DURING A FLOOD

Stay alert.

  • Move to higher ground when instructed by authorities.
  • Never walk, swim or drive through floodwater.
  • Keep away from swollen rivers, drains and fallen power lines.
  • Follow evacuation instructions and seek help when needed.
03✳
AFTER A FLOOD

Return carefully.

  • Return only when authorities say it is safe.
  • Avoid damaged buildings and electrical equipment exposed to water.
  • Drink safe water and avoid food contaminated by floodwater.
  • Report hazards and seek medical help if needed.

Remember: If you are in immediate danger, contact emergency services and follow official instructions.
