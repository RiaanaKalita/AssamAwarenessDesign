About us

I am Riaana from Assam, and therefore I deeply empathize with the intense challenges communities face during seasonal flooding. Growing up alongside the Brahmaputra river ecosystem highlights the urgent need for widespread education and early structural response options. Tide Turn was built to put actionable information directly into your hands.
