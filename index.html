Our Core Mission

Tide Turn is a localized educational database designed to bridge the structural knowledge gaps that place communities at risk during seasonal monsoons. By delivering clear, real-time warning indicators, verified support channels, and grade-appropriate safety drills, we empower multi-generational communities to turn anxiety into active resilience.

Why Preparedness Matters

Statistics indicate that early intervention planning drastically cuts down waterborne vulnerabilities and accidental trauma incidents. Knowing exactly which high grounds to target, maintaining an advance-packed emergency kit, and having authenticated public health lines saved inside your household eliminates emergency panic when seconds count.

About us

I am Riaana from Assam. Although I do not reside there directly, my extended family is spread across the state. Seeing the continuous hurdles they face during seasonal monsoons gives me a direct understanding and deep empathy for the region's challenges. Tide Turn was designed to ensure that families in high-risk zones always have actionable safety procedures right at their fingertips.
