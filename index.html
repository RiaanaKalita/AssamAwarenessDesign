Plan ahead, keep essentials ready and use official warnings rather than trying to judge water depth yourself.

WHY IT MATTERS

Floods affect more than homes.

Rising water can disrupt roads, schools, healthcare, farms and access to clean water. The impact varies by year and location.

57.5 lakh people reported affected in Assam in 2022
57.89 lakh people reported affected in Assam in 2020

Historical figures explain past impacts; they do not predict local conditions today.
