Why does Assam flood?

Assam lies in the Brahmaputra valley. In the monsoon, heavy rain makes the Brahmaputra and its tributaries rise and spill over their banks. Riverbank erosion, silt and a warming climate make the floods worse, and many families live right beside the river.

How floods change lives

Flooded streets trap vehicles and cut off roads.

Rescue teams use boats to reach families.

Families float belongings on rafts made of banana trunks.

Flood safety steps

⏪ Before a flood
  1. Find the nearest high ground and relief camp.
  2. Pack a waterproof emergency bag (see the checklist).
  3. Save emergency numbers in your phone and on paper.
  4. Pick a family meeting point.
🌊 During a flood
  1. Move to higher ground early. Do not wait.
  2. Never walk or drive through moving water: 6 inches can knock you down.
  3. Switch off the main power and gas if you can reach them safely.
  4. Listen to local authorities and the radio.
⏩ After a flood
  1. Return home only when authorities say it is safe.
  2. Stay away from standing water and fallen wires.
  3. Drink only boiled or purified water.
  4. Never run a generator indoors.
