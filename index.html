About Us

We created this website to help flood-affected communities in Assam and students in Grades 5–7 understand floods and stay safe. Our goal is to make reliable flood-safety information accessible and easy to understand through safety tips, health guidance, flood tracking and emergency resources. We believe that being informed and prepared can help people make safer decisions during floods.

BE READY, NOT UNPREPARED

Your Flood Safety Guide

Knowing what to do before, during and after a flood can help you make safer decisions and protect the people around you.

01
✓
BEFORE

Prepare early

  • Follow official weather forecasts and flood alerts.
  • Keep drinking water, a torch, essential medicines and important documents ready.
  • Know your household's evacuation route and safe meeting point.
  • Charge phones and save important emergency contacts.
  • Help children and older family members understand the plan.
02
!
DURING

Stay safe

  • Follow instructions from local authorities.
  • Move to safer ground when advised to evacuate.
  • Never walk, swim or drive through floodwater.
  • Stay away from electrical wires, drains and fast-flowing water.
  • Keep listening for official updates and alerts.
03
↗
AFTER

Return carefully

  • Return home only when authorities say it is safe.
  • Avoid damaged buildings and fallen power lines.
  • Do not drink flood-contaminated water; use safe water.
  • Wash hands and avoid food that may have been contaminated.
  • Ask local health services for help if you feel unwell.
!
Remember: never take risks to check a flood.

Do not approach rivers or cross floodwater to take photos, inspect water levels or retrieve belongings. Follow local emergency instructions.
